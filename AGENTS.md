# Repository instructions

- **This repository is public.** Commit source, fixtures a test reads and the README's screenshot. Screenshots, logs and receipts from a run stay in the ignored `.shots/` and `.pocket-build/`.
- Use Conventional Commits for commits and pull requests (`type(scope): summary`).
- Code, comments, tests, scripts and documentation are written in English. Chinese and Japanese text lives only in `src/i18n/`; `bun run check` fails on it anywhere else, including text drawn into a texture (draw a motif instead of characters).
- Every string a person sees goes through `src/i18n`: interface text under a key in `en.ts`, `zh.ts` and `ja.ts`, and catalogue names in English where the thing is defined, translated by id in `catalog.zh.ts` and `catalog.ja.ts`.
- The rules and the player's game are the Rust core, `crates/island-core` (`no_std`, no heap): the room's placement rules, the island's heights and footprints, walking and finding the way, walking in the house, the furniture handled by hand there (`handle.rs`: holding A at a piece to push, pull or turn it, Y to pick it up, a piece put down from the pockets), the pose, the door, the fish and their sight, the cast's float and the fishing line (`things.line`), and a fish's shadow (`OUTLINE`). A rule changes there; `src/core/*.ts` only hands the core the room and the island and asks it (through `src/core/abi.ts`), so the browser and the console run one implementation. A changed rule comes with a test in `test/rules.test.ts` or `test/island.test.ts` (they run on the core's WebAssembly) and, for the game, in the crate (`cargo test`). A new `pi_*` function is added to `ffi.rs` and `src/core/abi.ts`; `bun tools/bindings.ts` (run by the console's build) writes it into `programs/3ds-island/src/core.h` and `core_js.c`. What a host restates of the core's numbers (the codes in `src/core/*.ts`, the constants after `core.h`'s written functions, the room's and the island's sizes the interfaces draw with) is held to the crate by `test/core-mirror.test.ts`, which also fails when the bindings are not as `ffi.rs` writes them.
- The island is made by the recipe in `src/core/island.ts` (`RECIPE`, `makeIsland`), the same on every run and on the console; it is authoring, and stays in TypeScript. The tests check that every bit of land can be walked to from the door. Look at the map with `bun scripts/map.ts`.
- Mods (`src/mods/`) use only the surface in `src/mods/api.ts`, and run on the page and in the console's QuickJS alike; their words are messages in `src/i18n`.
- New furniture follows `docs/furniture-authoring.md`.
- What the player carries is defined in `src/items/items.ts` (by the core's numbers in `things.rs` `item`) and modelled in `src/items/models.ts`; a piece of furniture is carried by its number in `src/furniture/codes.ts` (a new piece is added at its end, never between) and shown by the catalogue's own picture and name (`carriedName`, `carriedThumb`, the console's `carriedPicture`); the export lowers the models the core lists (`THING_CODES` in `src/export/export.ts`) and the cook writes them to the pack's THNG table.
- The island's people talk through `src/npc/talk.ts` (shown by `src/ui/talk.ts` and `apps/island-3ds/talk.tsx`), as on the island games: lines in a speech box with the speaker's name, a question's answers to choose from, a line asking before any coins change hands (what a thing is and what it costs, what all of it fetches) and a goodbye when the player leaves. A new person follows these, as Mori does (`src/npc/mori.ts`).
- The shop's prices, stock and savings are the core's (`crates/island-core/src/shop.rs`). Its window in the browser (`src/ui/shop.ts`) and its sheet on the touch screen (`apps/island-3ds/shop.tsx`) are one design: a tab or a key added to one is added to the other.
- A save names the island's trees by count, so anything added to the recipe is added after what it scatters (as the shop's stall is): a change to the order of its random numbers moves every tree, and the saves no longer load.
- The core runs a host's first frame with no time (NaN): it does not call `f64::clamp` (which panics on NaN) but `math::clamp`.
- Look at what you changed: `bun run shot` takes a screenshot in headless Chrome, and `bun run e2e` drives the decorating flows with real input (both need `bun run dev`).

## The Nintendo 3DS

- The console draws what the browser draws. Its models, colours and pictures come from the export (`export.html`, `src/export/export.ts`) running the catalogue's own builders, and so does its light (`src/scene/lighting.ts` `look`, `outdoorLook`, the light's directions), the room's shell (`src/scene/shell.ts`) and how the view draws beyond the light (the pack's VIEW record: the camera, `src/scene/rig.ts` `RIG`; a lamp's halo, what is held, a piece's pop and the selection, `src/scene/view.ts`; the route's marker, `src/scene/route.ts` `MARKER`; the windows' stars, the house's windows, the sea floor); `tools/cook.ts` lowers them, and what it lowers for the console's screen alone (the island's night, the shadows it lays where the browser casts shadow maps) is written there. Do not model, colour or frame anything for the console alone: change the browser's, export it as a named constant the browser itself uses, then `bun tools/n3ds.ts build --export`; `test/core-mirror.test.ts` holds `pack.h` to the cook. What the interface already knows it hands the renderer (the house's windows' light, `island.house`; the music's fades, `island.mix`), and the core's numbers come through `core.h`.
- `tools/cook.ts` writes `island.pack` and `programs/3ds-island/src/pack.h` reads it. Change both in one commit and raise `PACK_VERSION` when a record changes.
- The console plays what the browser plays. The island's music and sounds are made from nothing by `src/audio/` (the synth, the pieces, the sounds, and `cues.ts`, which says what a frame's events sound like); the browser renders them in a worker, `tools/sound.ts` writes the same into the console's `sound.pack`, and `programs/3ds-island/src/audio.c` mixes them through the DSP. A sound or piece is added in `src/audio/names.ts` and made in `sfx.ts` or `music.ts`, never as a recording; its sound is heard through `cues.ts` or `hear.ts` on both. The console plays through the DSP when it has its firmware (`/3ds/dspfirm.cdc`), and otherwise through the sound hardware's own channels (CSND), which need none; `island.info().soundWay` says which.
- The console's GPU hangs on a draw of no triangles; the cook leaves them out (a part thinner than its error simplifies away) and simplifies the tools, seen close in hand, a quarter as hard.
- The island is to be reshaped as it is played, so nothing about its light is baked by the cook. A lamp costs a vertex it reaches twice the rest of its light in the shader, so the lamps' light on what stands still (the ground's chunks, the cells of props) is worked out on the console the first time each is drawn and kept with it (`scene.c` `light_variant`); whatever changes the island or its lamps calls `scene_relight`, and it is worked out again as it is next drawn. Only what moves takes the shader's lamps.
- A write to the console's SD card takes some 55 ms, three frames: `island.save` hands it to a writer thread and returns, and the interface keeps a large record (the island's things) only when it changes, never on a timer.
- After a change to the renderer, the island's models or the interface's frame, run `bun tools/n3ds.ts perf`: it measures the frame by the house, the stall and a bridge, by night and by day, and fails over its budget. Measure the frame there, not with `island.stats()` while commands are sent (they cost frames of their own).
- Triangles are what the console pays for (about 0.42 ms a thousand with the room's light), and so are draws: each costs the CPU its uniforms. A model that adds detail is measured on the console (`bun tools/n3ds.ts stats`) in its series' showroom. Outdoors the cook draws everything that stands still with the chunk it stands in; an island prop is held to `PROP_BUDGET` triangles.
- The player's phone is one design in the browser (`src/ui/phone.ts`) and on the touch screen (`apps/island-3ds/phone.tsx`): an app or a key added to one is added to the other. The route's dots are laid out once, in `src/island/route.ts`; the renderer's `draw_route` follows it.
- On the touch screen, the interface's drawing is paid per piece and per pixel (`bun tools/n3ds.ts stats`; its `js` is the whole tick, the GPU wait included). A rounded box is seven pieces; a rounded border larger than 64 px is drawn row by row every frame; a picture shrunk on the console thrashes the texture cache, so a picture is exported the size it is shown; a clipped box costs a scissor; and a full-screen layer is a layer of fill. What the renderer clears to is free.
- What an interface shows of the core's things (the pockets and the box, the tool in hand, the coins, the shop's stock, the field guide) is read again when `things.version()` moves, however they changed (a step's catch, a press, the shop): on the touch screen through `apps/island-3ds/outdoors.ts` (`slots`, `money`, `stock`, `guide`, made with `fromCore` and synced each frame), in the browser by the phone's redraws (`src/ui/phone.ts`). A sheet does not keep a copy of its own or read the core once.
- The interface (`apps/island-3ds`) runs the room's rules from `src/core`; it does not restate one. What decorating does to a room (a group's verdict, a turn, a nudge) is `src/core/edits.ts`, for both editors, and so is what the player does to it by hand (`handled`); what both phones lay out of the island (the house's tap area, the clock's colours, the day's icons) is `src/island/phone.ts`. Its text is `src/i18n` (the `ds.*` keys), and each language's glyphs are baked by `tools/fonts.ts`: run `bun apps/island-3ds/test/preview.ts <lang>` and look at every sheet before a device build.
- PocketJS is pinned in `vendor/pocketjs`. Do not edit the submodule for this app's behaviour: send the change to PocketJS and move the pin.
- The console is reached over its paired development connection (`bun tools/n3ds.ts install|push|capture|stats|input --host <address>`); the pairing key is a file under `vendor/pocketjs/.pocket/`, ignored by Git. Captures and logs stay in `.pocket-build/`.
